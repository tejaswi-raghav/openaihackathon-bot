WHAT CAN I ASK?

Public information belongs to the public.

Ask for records, documents, reports, or the status of government work. Avoid asking for opinions or explanations that are not already on record.

01

“How much money was sanctioned and spent on repairing the road in my village?”

Public works
02

“Please provide the beneficiary list for the housing scheme in my panchayat.”

“What action was taken on my complaint dated 12 June?”
